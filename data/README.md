# Car data

`challenger-3.6.json` lists the trims, standard features, and optional packages of the Dodge Challenger 3.6L V6 (SXT and GT) for model years 2015 to 2023, plus a glossary of every feature with a plain-language hint.

It is generated by `scripts/gen_challenger.py`. Edit the script, not the JSON, then run:

```
python3 data/scripts/gen_challenger.py data/challenger-3.6.json
```

## How much to trust it

Every trim and package has a `confidence` flag:

| Flag | Meaning | Years |
|---|---|---|
| `verified` | Taken from a published option list or press release | 2019 (SXT and GT AWD prices), 2020 SXT prices, 2017 GT base price and GT Interior Package |
| `likely` | From a secondary source (Cars.com, Autotrader) or carried over from a verified year | 2017-2018 SXT, 2020-2023 |
| `uncertain` | General knowledge, not yet checked | 2015-2016, most 2017-2018 packages |

Prices are US MSRP in USD excluding destination unless the notes say otherwise. A missing `priceUsd` means the price was not found.

## Known gaps

- No primary option lists yet for 2015-2018, 2021-2023, or GT package prices after 2019.
- 2023 base prices disagree by $1,420 between Autotrader and BuildPriceOption.
- Best next sources: window stickers (decoded by VIN) and Dodge order guides for each year.
- The Challenger never offered a 360-degree camera. It is in the glossary only because shoppers ask about it.
